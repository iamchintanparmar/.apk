Tkinter → APK Converter

Upload a Tkinter script. We convert it to Kivy (Android-capable) and try to build an APK.

Heads up: Tkinter itself can't run on Android — there's no Tcl/Tk build for it. This tool auto-converts common widgets (Label, Button, Entry, Frame, Checkbutton, Text) to Kivy equivalents. Anything it can't confidently translate is flagged, not silently dropped.
